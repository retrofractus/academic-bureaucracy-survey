Academic Bureaucracy Survey

Form ABS-1

Six questions about how long things take at your institution.

TimeAbout five minutes
ScopeUnited States
AnswersInstitution-level only

Before you start

  • This survey tracks quantitative information, not qualitative impressions. Please look up the actual dates in your email or your institution's system rather than estimating.
  • Answer for your most recent experience within the last 24 months. If it falls outside that window, say so rather than reaching further back — procurement systems change.
  • All durations are calendar days, not business days. In Google Sheets, =DATEDIF(A1,B1,"D") gives the days between two dates, where A1 is the earlier one.
  • Skipping questions is fine and expected. An honest "never" is more useful to me than a guess.
About you

Used once to verify that you work at the institution you named, and to reduce spam responses. The address is replaced with a one-way hash after verification and is never published or shared.

Getting money you have already been given
Consider the most recent grant you received for which you have both an award email and an email notifying you the funds were available internally.

How many days passed between the award notification email and the moment you first had access to an active account or index number you could spend from?

Consider the most recent reimbursement you submitted for which you have both the submission date and the payout date.

How many days passed from when you submitted your receipts to when the money reached you?

Buying things
Consider your most recent work purchase that was not a reimbursement, and for which you have evidence of the submission and approval dates.

How many days passed from when you submitted the purchase order request to when you were notified the PO had been approved?

How many distinct forms, documents, or certifications must a new vendor provide to be added to your institution's purchasing system?

Answer for the process as it stands today, for a typical US-based small business selling standard goods or services. Do not answer for a foreign entity, an individual contractor, or a sole-source purchase, which normally require extra paperwork. Count only what the vendor themselves must supply: W-9, certificate of insurance, banking or ACH form, supplier registration, financial statements, compliance attestations.

And how many must you personally complete or help complete for that same vendor to be added?

For example, sole-source justifications, requisition forms, or internal approval memos.

Hiring people
Consider the most recent US-based person you hired or helped hire into a research or staff role.

Exclude anyone who needed visa sponsorship — that delay belongs to the federal government, not your university. How many days passed from when the candidate accepted the offer to their first day of work?

Anything else

Received. Thank you.

Your institution's row gets closer to being publishable with every response. The single most useful thing you can do now is send this to two colleagues at your university — the results only work if enough people at one place answer.
